About

Being wrong is data

Most tools stop at "correct" or "incorrect." That's the least useful part of a mistake. The interesting part is why a wrong answer felt right in the moment — that's usually where the actual misconception is hiding.

Explain My Mistake is a small, single-page tool built around that idea: paste what you were asked and what you answered, and get a plain-language breakdown of the gap between them.

the goal was never the right answer — it's knowing why the wrong one felt right.
